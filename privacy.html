Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy describes how Turncast Publisher ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is a private, internal tool operated by the band Turncast to publish and manage videos on the band's own social media accounts. It is not offered to or used by the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

  • Basic information about the Turncast YouTube channel (channel name, ID).
  • Videos on the Turncast channel and their metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status).
  • Public statistics for the Turncast channel's own videos (views, likes, comment counts).
  • Video files the band uploads, taken from the band's own cloud storage.

The app only accesses the Turncast channel, through a sign-in the channel owner grants with Google OAuth. It does not access other users' YouTube accounts or data.

How the information is used

Solely to upload and manage the band's own videos and to review how the band's own content performs. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and service providers

Access tokens are held by our integration provider, Composio, in order to make API requests on the band's behalf. Performance notes we keep are stored privately in the band's own files. Statistics are refreshed from YouTube rather than stored long-term, and any stored statistics are deleted or refreshed within 30 days.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, we delete any stored data obtained through YouTube API Services within 7 days. You can also request deletion by contacting us below.
