Turncast Publisher

Turncast Publisher is a private, internal tool used only by the band to publish and manage our own videos on our own social channels. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Turncast YouTube channel, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read basic statistics for our own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts or data.

By using features that rely on YouTube API Services, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
